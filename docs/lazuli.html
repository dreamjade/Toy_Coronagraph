Lazuli telescope (prysm)#

The Lazuli space telescope (UASAL; its configuration packages call it “Ultramarine”, UM), computed with prysm.

[[coronagraph]]
type = "lazuli"
charge = 0                  # 0: the telescope alone; even >= 2: a vortex mask and a Lyot stop
wavelengths = ["632 nm"]    # "auto": [observation] wavelength
throughput = "auto"         # weights from the mirror, filter and detector curves of the configs

The telescope and its wide-field coronagraph camera (WCC) come from the UASAL configuration packages, read once (lazuli_parameters): config_um (focal length f_eff, mirror coatings) and config_um_wcc (pupil sampling, beam size on M1, detector, filter). The pupil (pupil()) is a circle of diameter D = the beam size on M1 (3.0 m) with an anti-aliased (gray) edge, filling pupil_samples x pupil_samples samples (x along the columns, y along the rows, centred on the array). OPD: an optional wavefront error map (opd) adds Lazuli’s aberrations. Without one (the default) the optics are ideal: a perfect telescope and, with a charge, an ideal coronagraph. The mirror OPD maps of the configs ($UASAL_ARCHIVE) are not bundled.

A point source at angle (theta_x, theta_y), one wavelength at a time (LazuliBackend.source):

pupil field   A exp(2 pi i (OPD + theta_x x + theta_y y) / lambda);
coronagraph   charge >= 2: an ideal vortex (prysm's multi-resolution focal-plane propagation)
              then a circular Lyot stop of lyot_mask_size D; charge 0: nothing;
image         prysm's matrix DFT (MDFT) onto the image pixels; a pixel is pixel_scale scene
              lambda/D, i.e. pixel_scale (wavelength0 / diameter0) f_eff in the focal plane
              (only the angle matters).

The image is the weighted sum over the wavelengths. The image size must be odd: the star then sits on the central pixel, which is prysm’s centre n//2 as well as toycoronagraph’s (n-1)/2, so the images compare with prysm’s pixel by pixel. Intensities follow toycoronagraph.optics: each pixel holds |field|^2 / sum(A^2), the fraction of the source’s light entering the aperture (prysm’s executor is unitary), so an unocculted source sums to 1 over the whole focal plane at every wavelength. The focal field of P pupil samples repeats every P lambda/D (Lazuli’s, at each wavelength): every source is repeated P lambda/D away along x and y. optics_from_table refuses a sampling whose period at the shortest wavelength does not clear the PSF frames (pupil samples are cheap; aliasing is not).

The Lazuli team’s get_psf is here too (get_psf, on prysm 0.22, its inputs from tele_data), with the wavelengths in um, prysm’s unit (their version multiplies them by 1e-3, so it needs nm). It is charge 0 of the backend:

tele_data['Aperture'], ['dx'], ['fnum']   pupil(), its sample D / pupil_samples, f_eff / D
tele_data['OPD']                          opd (a map in nm)
tele_data['Pixsize'] / oversample         the image pixel: [grid] pixel_scale (detector_pixel_scale()
                                          gives the WCC detector's)
tele_data['Wavelengths']                  wavelengths: um there; m here, or text ("0.632 um", "632 nm")
tele_data['Throughput']                   throughput ("auto": spectral_weights)
shift                                     moves get_psf's focal grid by +shift; here the source moves
                                          instead, to -shift

get_psf normalises each wavelength’s PSF and the weighted sum to 1 over its own image; the backend normalises each wavelength to the light entering the aperture, which keeps the light falling outside the image (and the coronagraph’s throughput).

This is the only module importing prysm and the config packages; toycoronagraph imports it when a “lazuli” coronagraph is used (Optics.lazuli names it as its module).

The Lazuli telescope and WCC parameters, read once from config_um and config_um_wcc.

Returns:

  • diameter (float): pupil diameter D in m: the beam size on M1 (config_um_wcc E2ES_default.optics.m1.beam_size; M1’s clear aperture is larger, 3.065 m).

  • focal_length (float): effective focal length in m (config_um telescope.general.f_eff, at the centre of the field; config_um’s f_number = 15 is a provisional design requirement).

  • pupil_samples (int): pupil samples across D (config_um_wcc E2ES_default.simulation.beam_sampling).

  • pixel_size (float): WCC detector pixel size in m (config_um_wcc common_params.sensor.pixel_size).

  • curves (dict): {name: (wavelengths (n,) float64 in m, values (n,) float64)} for “m1” (aluminium reflectance), “m2”, “m3”, “m4” (silver reflectance), “filter” (SDSS r’) and “qe” (detector).

Return type:

SimpleNamespace

toycoronagraph.lazuli.spectral_weights(wavelengths)[source]#

The relative weight of each wavelength through Lazuli and the WCC: M1 aluminium x M2-M4 silver reflectance x SDSS r’ filter x detector QE, interpolated at the wavelengths (0 outside a curve), times the width of the band each wavelength stands for (halfway to its neighbours, as far again beyond the first and last: np.gradient of the sorted wavelengths). The weighted sum is then the band integral however the band is sampled; evenly spaced wavelengths get the curves’ product alone.

Parameters:

wavelengths (sequence of float) – wavelengths in m, in any order (one wavelength: weight 1).

Returns:

(n,) float64 weights, summing to 1.

Return type:

np.ndarray

Raises:

ValueError – no light at any of the wavelengths (all outside the r’ band, about 540-730 nm).

The Optics of a “lazuli” [[coronagraph]] table, its “auto” settings resolved.

  • img_pixel (int) – Number of pixels on one edge of the image (odd).

  • psf_range (float) – Half-width of the image, in scene lambda/D.

  • wavelength (float) – The scene’s wavelength in m ([observation] wavelength; “auto” wavelengths use it).

  • diameter (float) – The scene’s telescope diameter in m ([observation] diameter).

  • size_fix (str) – How the user makes the image size odd, with two %d for the odd sizes around img_pixel, e.g. “set [grid] size = %d or %d (the image is resampled onto it)”.

Return type:

Optics

Raises:

ParamError – img_pixel is even, or the focal field’s period (pupil samples x Lazuli’s lambda/D at the shortest wavelength) does not clear the PSF frames: the farthest pixel of a star-centred radial frame (psf.extended_frame_size) from a source at the image edge, (N-1)/2 + (M-1)/2 pixels.

Warns:

  • SamplingWarning – fewer than MIN_SAMPLING x 8/3 pupil samples per lambda/D of the image’s half-width (the PSFs’ far wings come out too low near the image edge; see optics.MIN_SAMPLING).

  • OSError – the OPD map cannot be read (e.g. FileNotFoundError).

class toycoronagraph.lazuli.LazuliBackend(optics)[source]#

Bases: object

The Lazuli telescope, alone (charge 0) or with an ideal vortex coronagraph (see the module docstring).

A source enters only through the tilt across the pupil, whose width is D: for a fixed image pixel the field is a Fourier integral over the pupil, so the intensity is band-limited in the source angle at D / lambda cycles per radian, i.e. (D / diameter0) (wavelength0 / lambda) cycles per scene lambda/D. Without an OPD map, the pupil and the stops are circles: rotationally symmetric PSFs.

Variables:

  • optics (Optics) – kind ‘lazuli’ (see Optics.lazuli).

  • rotationally_symmetric (bool) – True without an OPD map.

  • bandlimit (float) – (D / diameter0) (wavelength0 / shortest wavelength), cycles per scene lambda/D.

  • lambda_d (float) – the scene’s lambda/D, wavelength0 / diameter0, in radians.

  • pupil_dx (float) – pupil sample, in mm.

  • efl (float) – focal length (config_um’s f_eff), in mm.

  • um_per_lambda_d (float) – focal-plane microns per scene lambda/D.

  • x,​y (np.ndarray) – (P, P) float64 pupil coordinates in mm (x along the columns, y along the rows).

  • aperture (np.ndarray) – (P, P) float64 circle of diameter D (anti-aliased edge).

  • opd (np.ndarray) – (P, P) float64 OPD in nm (zeros without a map).

  • lyot_stop (np.ndarray or None) – (P, P) float64 circle of diameter lyot_mask_size D; None for charge 0.

  • entering (float) – sum(aperture^2), the light entering the aperture (the intensity unit).

  • lyot_light (float) – sum((aperture lyot_stop)^2), a source’s light after the Lyot stop without the vortex (the throughput’s reference; for charge 0, entering).

  • vortex (callable or None) – prysm’s vortex_phase_mask(charge); None for charge 0.

  • channels (list) – per wavelength, (wavelength in um, weight, multi-resolution vortex executor or None).

  • frame_executors (list) – per wavelength, the MDFT onto the whole image.

Parameters:

optics (Optics) – An Optics of kind ‘lazuli’ (see Optics.lazuli).

The Lazuli team’s get_psf on prysm 0.22 (prepare_executor + focus_dft replace focus_fixed_sampling; numpy instead of cupy), with tele_data[‘Wavelengths’] in um (their version multiplies the wavelengths by 1e-3, so it needs them in nm).
