Optics and PSFs
===============

An optical system gives the image of a point source (:doc:`optics`: vortex coronagraphs with hcipy; :doc:`lazuli`
with prysm). From those images the package makes the final image of a target: the circular method for circularly
symmetric targets and optics, the spatial method for any target (:doc:`psf`, :doc:`spatial`).
